Our story

A ball of yarn, a lot of patience, and a love of making.

Every stitch has a story

Tangled Tales began with a single ball of yarn and a crochet hook. What started as a way to make gifts for friends and family grew into a small shop that makes cozy things for other people's homes.

Every piece is still made by hand, one loop at a time. We work in small batches, check each piece before it's packed, and add a handwritten note to every order.

Soft, lasting yarns

Caring for your crochet

A little care keeps handmade pieces looking new. Every order comes with a printed care card.

Wash gently
Hand wash in cool water with mild soap. Don't wring or twist.
Dry flat
Press out water in a towel, then reshape and dry flat. Hanging can stretch it.
Keep away from heat
No tumble dryer or direct sun, which can fade and shrink the yarn.
Store folded
Fold and keep in a cool, dry place. Avoid hanging heavy pieces.
