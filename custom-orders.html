Custom orders

Tell us your idea and we'll make it real.

How it works

  1. Tell us your ideaFill in the form. Photos and rough sketches help, and you can send them on WhatsApp.
  2. We send a sketch and a quoteWithin two days, we reply with a sketch, a price and a delivery time.
  3. We stitch and shipOnce you approve, we start work and send progress photos. Most pieces take 2 to 3 weeks.

Colours
Pick from our full yarn range, or send a photo to match.
Size
Bigger, smaller or made to fit a person or pet.
Names and initials
Stitched on for birthdays, weddings and new babies.
Gift packing
Wrapped, tied and sent with a handwritten note.
