Frequently asked questions

How do I order?

Add pieces to your cart, then send your order to us on WhatsApp or by email. We confirm the total, delivery time and payment details with you before anything is made or shipped.

How long does delivery take?

Ready-made pieces ship in about 3 to 5 days. Custom pieces usually take 2 to 3 weeks. We will always confirm the timing with you first.

What payment methods do you accept?

Can I return a handmade item?

Because each piece is made to order, we cannot accept returns on custom pieces. If a ready-made piece arrives damaged or not as described, message us within 3 days and we will make it right. Replace this with your own policy.

How should I wash my crochet?

Hand wash in cool water with mild soap and dry flat. There is more on our story page.

Can I send you a photo of what I want?

Yes, please do. Send it on WhatsApp or by email, or mention it in a custom order request.
